## Customer

Jespr is a Swiss technology startup focused on cycling. The company designs,
engineers and manufactures an innovative bike computer in Switzerland, featuring
an intuitive design, high-quality data visualization and best-in-class mapping
capabilities. As the first bike computer with a built-in SIM card for a
permanent mobile connection, Jespr positions itself as a serious alternative to
established brands such as Garmin and Wahoo.

## Collaboration

bespinian supports Jespr as a technology partner in software development and
cloud computing, building the digital platform around the bike computer.
